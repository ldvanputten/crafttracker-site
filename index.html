CraftTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Short version: CraftTracker collects nothing. Everything you enter (projects, photos, yarn and fabric details, notes) stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party.

What the app stores

CraftTracker keeps your projects, row/step counts, yarn and fabric charts, progress photos, and notes in a local database on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data is never transmitted anywhere by the app.

What we collect

Nothing. CraftTracker has no network code, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting
  • No advertising identifiers or trackers
  • No data is uploaded to a server; there is no server

Camera and photo library access

CraftTracker asks for camera and photo library permission only so you can attach photos to your projects, yarn, fabric, and progress timeline, and so a finished timelapse can be saved back to your photo library. Photos you add are stored in the app's local database on your device, exactly like the rest of your project data, not uploaded anywhere.

Sharing

If you use the Share Sheet to export a timelapse or a photo, that's a direct action you take with iOS's standard sharing tools (Messages, Save to Photos, AirDrop, etc.); CraftTracker itself is not a party to that transfer.

Data deletion

Since everything lives only on your device, deleting a project (or deleting the app itself) removes that data permanently. There is no copy stored elsewhere to delete.

Children's privacy

CraftTracker collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional iCloud sync), this page will be updated and the date above will reflect it.
